# SkinVidhi quiz

Target: about one minute. One question per screen, one tap per answer where possible.
Every question must change the routine; if it doesn't, it doesn't belong here.

| # | Question | Type | Options | Effect on the routine |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What's your skin type? | one choice | Oily, Dry, Combination, Normal, Not sure | Moisturizer texture (gel vs cream) and cleanser type. "Not sure" opens a helper: "How does your skin feel an hour after washing?" (tight = dry, shiny all over = oily, shiny T-zone = combination, comfortable = normal). |
| 2 | What would you most like to improve? | up to 2; first pick is the priority | Breakouts, Dark spots & marks, Uneven tone / discoloration, Fine lines, Redness, Dullness & texture, Dryness / dehydration, Flaky or peeling patches, Oiliness & pores | Chooses the night treatment. "Flaky or peeling patches" switches to barrier repair (ceramides; no strong acids or retinoids) and shows the dermatologist note below. |
| 3 | How easily does your skin react to products? | one choice | Rarely, Sometimes, Often | "Often" filters out fragrance, essential oils and strong actives. |
| 4 | Anything you know you should avoid? (optional) | any | Fragrance, Essential oils, Alcohol, Nuts, Soy, None / not sure | Hard filters on ingredients. |
| 5 | Have you used retinol or exfoliating acids before? | one choice | Never, A little, Yes, regularly | Strength of actives (beginner retinol vs 1% retinol / adapalene). |
| 6 | Are you pregnant or breastfeeding? | one choice | Yes, No, Prefer not to say | "Yes" excludes retinoids. "Prefer not to say" changes nothing. |
| 7 | What's your budget for the whole routine? | slider | $40 to $200+ | Filters on the upfront total; results also show a monthly cost. |
| 8 | Where do you live? | city search | autocomplete | Climate: high UV means stronger SPF, humidity means lighter textures. |
| 9 | Your skin tone (optional) | swatch | 6 tones, Fitzpatrick-style | Deeper tones: no-white-cast sunscreens, extra care against dark marks. |

## On the results page, not in the quiz

Filters the user can toggle after seeing their routine: fragrance-free only, US-approved sunscreens only
(no imports), matte or dewy finish.

## Wording notes

- US spelling throughout ("discoloration", "color").
- "Dark spots & marks" = individual spots and post-acne marks; "Uneven tone / discoloration" = patchy or
  overall uneven color (e.g. melasma). Both lead to brightening treatments.

## Safety notes shown with results

- **Flaky or peeling patches:** "If patches are itchy, scaly, spreading, or don't improve within 2–3 weeks,
  see a dermatologist. This can be a skin condition (such as eczema, psoriasis or seborrheic dermatitis)
  rather than dryness."
- SkinVidhi is not medical advice; this note is shown whenever a concern can point to a medical condition.
